Free online calculators for everyday money, time and health

EverydayCalc is a growing set of free calculators for the questions people ask every week. How many hours did I work this week, and did I earn overtime? What will my paycheck be after taxes? What is my BMI, and what weight range counts as healthy for my height? Each calculator sits above a plain-English guide that explains the formula, shows examples, answers common questions and links to official sources such as the U.S. Department of Labor, the IRS and the CDC.

The tools are written and maintained by one person, Muddasir Riaz, and we add new calculators and guides regularly. If you spot a mistake or want a calculator we do not have yet, contact us.

Frequently asked questions

Are the EverydayCalc calculators free?

Yes. Every calculator is free to use, with no sign-up and no account.

Is my information saved when I use a calculator?

No. The calculations run in your browser. The numbers you enter are not sent to our servers or stored by us.

How accurate are the results?

Each calculator uses a standard, published formula and shows it on the page, along with worked examples and links to official sources. Results are estimates for information only and are not financial, tax, legal or medical advice.

Which calculators are coming next?

We are adding new calculators for work and pay, such as overtime, hourly to salary and time card calculators. Each one will come with its own guide.
