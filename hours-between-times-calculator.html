Hours Between Times Calculator

By Muddasir Riaz · Updated November 12, 2026

Enter a start time and an end time to see the hours and minutes between them, in decimal hours too. Add an unpaid break to see your hours worked.

If the end time is earlier than the start time, we treat it as the next day (for example 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM).

Quick answer: To find the hours between two times, subtract the start time from the end time. 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM is 9 hours. With a 30-minute unpaid lunch it is 8.5 hours, and with a 60-minute lunch it is 8 hours. 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM is also 9 hours.

How to use the hours between two times calculator

  1. Enter the start time and end time. Pick AM or PM in the time box, or use the 24-hour clock.
  2. Add a break if you want it taken off. Type the unpaid minutes, such as 30 or 60.
  3. Press Calculate hours. You see the hours and minutes, the decimal hours, and the total minutes.

How many hours is 8 to 5?

8:00 AM to 5:00 PM is 9 hours. Count from 8 AM to 12 noon (4 hours) and from 12 noon to 5 PM (5 hours). If you take a 30-minute unpaid lunch, you work 8.5 hours. With a 60-minute unpaid lunch you work 8 hours, which is a standard full workday.

How do you calculate the time between two times?

Hours between = end time − start time

  1. Change both times to the 24-hour clock. 5:00 PM becomes 17:00. 8:00 AM stays 8:00.
  2. Subtract the start time from the end time. 17:00 − 8:00 = 9:00, which is 9 hours.
  3. If minutes do not line up, borrow an hour. From 8:45 AM to 5:15 PM: 17:15 − 8:45 = 8 hours 30 minutes.
  4. Take off any unpaid break.

How do you calculate hours across noon or midnight?

Across noon, nothing special is needed once you use the 24-hour clock. Across midnight, add 24 hours to the end time first. For example, 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM is 6:00 + 24:00 = 30:00, and 30:00 − 22:00 = 8 hours. The calculator does this when the end time is earlier than the start time.

How do you convert minutes to decimal hours?

Payroll uses decimal hours, so 8 hours 30 minutes is written as 8.5. Divide the minutes by 60 and add them to the hours.

Common mistakes when calculating hours between times

  • Mixing up AM and PM. 8 to 5 means 8 AM to 5 PM. If you type 5 AM by mistake the answer will look strange.
  • Forgetting the break. A time span is not the same as paid time if lunch is unpaid.
  • Using 100 minutes per hour. There are 60 minutes in an hour, so convert minutes carefully.
  • Ignoring midnight. For night shifts, the end time is on the next day.

How do I calculate hours between two times?

Convert both times to the 24-hour clock, subtract the start time from the end time, and take off any unpaid break. For example, 8:45 AM to 5:15 PM is 8 hours 30 minutes.

How do I calculate the hours between two times overnight?

Add 24 hours to the end time, then subtract the start time. 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM is 8 hours.

How do I convert hours and minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60 and add them to the hours. 8 hours 30 minutes is 8.5 hours, and 7 hours 45 minutes is 7.75 hours.
