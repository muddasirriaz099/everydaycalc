How to use the Paycheck Calculator

  1. Enter your gross pay for one paycheck.
  2. Select how often you are paid.
  3. Enter your federal and state tax rates (your pay stub shows the withheld amounts).
  4. Add deductions and press Calculate.

The formula

Taxable pay = gross - pre-tax deductions. Federal tax and state tax are taken as a percentage of taxable pay. FICA is 7.65% of taxable pay for Social Security and Medicare. Net pay = taxable pay - federal - state - FICA - after-tax deductions.

Example

Gross $2,000 biweekly, 12% federal, 4% state, no deductions: federal $240, state $80, FICA $153, take-home $1,527 per paycheck, about $39,702 per year.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is take-home pay calculated?

Take-home pay = gross pay minus pre-tax deductions, federal income tax, state income tax, and FICA (Social Security 6.2% plus Medicare 1.45%), minus any after-tax deductions.

What is FICA?

FICA is the US payroll tax that funds Social Security and Medicare. Employees pay 7.65% of wages (6.2% Social Security up to an annual wage cap, plus 1.45% Medicare).

Why is my real paycheck different from this estimate?

Actual withholding depends on your W-4, filing status, state and local taxes, benefits and other deductions. Use this as an estimate and check your pay stub for exact numbers.
