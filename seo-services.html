Do you build websites and tools too?

Yes, small ones. MrCalcs itself is built this way: I direct AI coding tools (often called vibe coding), then test every result, fix mistakes and check the numbers against official sources. That means I can turn an idea into a simple, fast, SEO-ready page or tool, such as a landing page, a calculator or a small content site, and I know how to make it indexable from day one. How do you work?

  1. Understand your goal. Who is your customer and what should they do on your site?
  2. Audit and research. I check your pages, Search Console data, competitors and keywords.
  3. Prioritise. You get a short list sorted by effort and likely impact, not a 100-point dump.
  4. Fix and report. I make or guide the changes, then report what happened in plain language.

What I will not do

  • I will not promise first-page rankings or fixed traffic numbers.
  • I will not buy links, use link farms or copy other sites' content.
  • I will not tell you something is working when the data says it is not.

Frequently asked questions

Who do you work with?

Small websites, blogs, local businesses and online tools that want a clear, honest look at their SEO. I am a junior specialist, so I am a good fit for small projects, audits and learning-focused work rather than large enterprise sites.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee a Google ranking. What I can promise is a clear list of what to fix, why it matters, and honest reporting of what changes. My own project, MrCalcs, shows what a new site really looks like, including the parts that did not work.

What do I get from an SEO audit?

A written report that covers on-page SEO (titles, headings, content, internal links), technical basics (indexing, sitemap, speed, mobile), search intent and keyword gaps, plus a short, prioritised list of fixes.

Can you build a small website or tool for me?

Yes, simple ones such as landing pages, calculators and small content sites, built with AI coding tools and then tested by me. They are structured for SEO from the start: clean titles, schema, sitemap and fast mobile pages. I am not a replacement for a full development team on complex software.

Do you use AI?

Yes, as a helper for drafting and building, and I check the result myself against Google Search Console data and official sources. I do not publish AI text without review.

How do we work together?

You message me with your site and goal. I reply with what I can do and how long it will take. After the work, you get a report and I stay available for follow-up questions.

Are you open to internships or trial projects?

Yes. I am open to small projects, junior SEO roles and internships where I can gain real client experience.
