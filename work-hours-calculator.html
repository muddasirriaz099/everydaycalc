Work Hours Calculator

Find your daily and weekly hours, overtime and gross pay.

Overtime estimate uses 1.5x pay after 40 hours per week (federal rule). State rules may differ.

How to use the Work Hours Calculator

  1. Enter the time you clocked in and clocked out.
  2. Enter your unpaid break in minutes (for example 30 or 60).
  3. Enter how many days you work per week.
  4. Add your hourly rate to see estimated gross pay, then press Calculate.

The formula

Hours worked = (Clock out - Clock in) - Break. Weekly hours = daily hours x days per week. Any weekly hours above 40 are shown as overtime paid at 1.5x your rate.

Example

Clock in 9:00 AM, clock out 6:00 PM, 60 minute break, $20 per hour, 5 days: daily hours = 8.00, weekly hours = 40.00, gross weekly pay = $800.00.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate work hours with a lunch break?

Subtract your clock-in time from your clock-out time, then subtract the unpaid break. Example: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM is 8 hours; minus a 60 minute lunch gives 7 hours.

How is overtime calculated in the USA?

Under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act, most non-exempt employees must be paid at least 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek. Some states have additional daily overtime rules, so check your state.

Does this calculator handle night shifts?

Yes. If your clock-out time is earlier than your clock-in time, it treats the shift as ending the next day, for example 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
