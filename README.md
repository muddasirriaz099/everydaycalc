# EverydayCalc

Free online calculators for work, pay and health. Simple answers, with the formula shown. No sign-up.

**Live site:** https://muddasirriaz099.github.io/everydaycalc/

## Calculators

| Calculator | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Work Hours Calculator](https://muddasirriaz099.github.io/everydaycalc/work-hours-calculator.html) | Daily and weekly hours with unpaid breaks and overnight shifts |
| [Time Card Calculator](https://muddasirriaz099.github.io/everydaycalc/time-card-calculator.html) | Weekly timesheet: total hours, overtime and estimated pay |
| [Overtime Calculator](https://muddasirriaz099.github.io/everydaycalc/overtime-calculator.html) | Time and a half and double time, federal and California rules |
| [BMI Calculator](https://muddasirriaz099.github.io/everydaycalc/bmi-calculator.html) | BMI in feet/pounds or cm/kg with healthy weight range |
| [Paycheck Calculator](https://muddasirriaz099.github.io/everydaycalc/paycheck-calculator.html) | Estimated take-home pay after tax, FICA and deductions |

## About

Built by [Muddasir Riaz](https://github.com/muddasirriaz099). Each calculator shows its formula, a worked example and official sources (US Department of Labor, CDC, IRS).

These tools give estimates for information only. They are not legal, tax or medical advice.
