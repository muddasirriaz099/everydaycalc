BMI Calculator - ft/inch & lbs (USA)

How to Use

  • Enter height in ft + inch (USA style)
  • Enter weight in lbs
  • Click Calculate

Advantage & Disadvantage

Advantage
USA standard, fast health status		Disadvantage
Not accurate for athletes, muscle weight

Formula

BMI = 703 x lbs / (total inches)^2. Example: 5'9 = 69 inch, 160 lbs = BMI 23.6 Normal
