BMI Calculator

By Muddasir Riaz · Updated October 31, 2026

Check your body mass index in US units (feet, inches, pounds) or metric (cm, kg). You get your BMI, your weight category and the healthy weight range for your height.

For adults aged 20 and older. BMI is a screening number, not a diagnosis. For children and teens, use the CDC's age-and-sex-specific BMI percentile tools.

Quick answer: BMI (body mass index) = 703 × weight in pounds ÷ height in inches squared. In metric, BMI = weight in kg ÷ height in meters squared. For adults, a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is in the healthy weight range, 25 to 29.9 is overweight, and 30 or more is in the obesity range.

  3. Press Calculate BMI. You will see your BMI number, your weight category and the weight range that counts as healthy for your height.

What is BMI?

BMI stands for body mass index. It is a number that compares your weight with your height. Because it only needs two measurements, doctors and public health agencies use it as a quick screening tool to spot weight categories that may be linked with health risks. BMI does not measure body fat directly, and it does not tell you how healthy you are by itself.

BMI formula

US units: BMI = 703 × weight (lb) ÷ height (in)²

Metric units: BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²

Take a person who is 5 ft 7 in (67 inches) and weighs 160 pounds. Square the height: 67 × 67 = 4,489. Then 703 × 160 = 112,480. Divide 112,480 by 4,489 and you get a BMI of 25.1. In metric the same person is about 170 cm and 72.6 kg: 72.6 ÷ (1.70 × 1.70) = 72.6 ÷ 2.89, which is about 25.1.

BMI categories for adults

These cutoffs are used by the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) for adults aged 20 and older.

How do you calculate BMI in pounds and inches?

Multiply your weight in pounds by 703. Separately, multiply your height in inches by itself. Divide the first number by the second. For a person who is 5 ft 4 in (64 inches) and 130 pounds, that is 703 × 130 = 91,390, divided by 64 × 64 = 4,096, which is 22.3. That is in the healthy range. Do not forget to include the feet when you convert to inches: 5 feet 4 inches is 5 × 12 + 4 = 64 inches.

What is a good BMI for adults?

For most adults, a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered the healthy weight range. People in this range have a lower average risk of some weight-related conditions than people at either end. That is only an average. A person at a BMI of 26 who is active, eats well and has normal blood pressure can be healthier than someone at 23 who has other risk factors. BMI is one number, and your doctor looks at many more.

Limits of BMI

  • It does not separate muscle from fat. Athletes and people with a lot of muscle can have a high BMI without having extra body fat.
  • It does not show where fat is stored. Fat around the waist is linked with higher health risk than fat in other places. Measures such as waist circumference can add context.
  • Age changes the picture. Older adults tend to lose muscle and may have more body fat at the same BMI as a younger person.
  • Different groups may have different risk levels. Some health organizations use different cutoffs for some ethnic groups.
  • It is not for children and teens. For ages 2 to 19, the CDC compares BMI with other children of the same age and sex, using percentiles instead of the adult cutoffs.
  • It is not for pregnancy. Weight guidance during pregnancy is different, so ask your doctor or midwife.

What should you do with your BMI result?

Treat the number as a starting point for a conversation, not a verdict. If your BMI is in the healthy range, you do not need to change anything because of the number alone. If it is above or below the healthy range, a doctor can look at your whole health picture, which may include blood pressure, blood sugar, cholesterol, family history, activity and eating habits. Do not start a strict diet or a big change in exercise only because of a BMI calculator. If you have concerns about your weight or how you eat, talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian.

BMI in metric units: a quick example

Metric is easy if you keep the units straight. A person who is 175 cm tall and weighs 80 kg is 1.75 meters tall. Square the height to get 3.0625. Divide 80 by 3.0625 and the result is 26.1, which is in the overweight category. To convert, 1 inch is 2.54 centimeters and 1 pound is about 0.4536 kilograms, so you can use whichever units you know best.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI for adults?

A BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is considered the healthy weight range for adults aged 20 and older. 25 to 29.9 is overweight, and 30 or higher is in the obesity range, according to the CDC categories.

How do I calculate BMI in pounds and inches?

BMI = 703 x weight in pounds / (height in inches squared). For example, 160 lb at 5 ft 7 in (67 in) gives 703 x 160 / 4,489 = 25.1.

How do I calculate BMI in kg and cm?

Convert centimeters to meters by dividing by 100, square the result, and divide your weight in kilograms by it. For example, 70 kg and 170 cm gives 70 / (1.7 x 1.7) = 24.2.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. BMI does not separate muscle from fat, so athletes can score high. It also does not show where fat is stored and is less reliable for older adults. It is a screening tool, not a diagnosis.

What BMI is considered obese?

A BMI of 30 or higher is in the obesity range for adults. It is split into Class 1 (30 to 34.9), Class 2 (35 to 39.9) and Class 3 (40 or higher).

What is the BMI formula?

In US units, BMI = 703 x pounds / inches squared. In metric, BMI = kilograms / meters squared. Both give the same result for the same person.

Does BMI change with age or sex for adults?

The adult cutoffs are the same for men and women aged 20 and older. For children and teens, BMI is compared with percentiles for their age and sex instead.

Can I use this BMI calculator for my child?

No. This calculator uses adult categories. For children and teens aged 2 to 19, use the CDC's child and teen BMI percentile calculator or ask your pediatrician.
