Formulas

US: BMI = 703 x pounds / inches². Metric: BMI = kg / meters².

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults, a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is considered in the healthy range. 25 to 29.9 is overweight and 30 or above is obese, according to standard CDC categories.

How do I calculate BMI in pounds and inches?

BMI = 703 x weight in pounds / (height in inches squared). Example: 160 lb at 5 ft 7 in (67 in) gives 703 x 160 / 4489 = 25.1.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. BMI does not separate muscle from fat, so athletes can score high. It is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Talk to a doctor about your health.
