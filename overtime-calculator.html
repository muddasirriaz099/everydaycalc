How to use the Overtime Calculator

  1. Enter your regular hourly rate.
  2. Enter the total hours you worked this week.
  3. Choose the overtime multiplier (1.5x or 2x).
  4. Press Calculate to see regular pay, overtime pay and your total.

Overtime formula

Overtime pay = overtime hours × hourly rate × multiplier.
Total pay = regular hours × rate + overtime pay.

Example

Rate $20, 50 hours, 1.5x: regular = 40 × $20 = $800. Overtime = 10 × $20 × 1.5 = $300. Total = $1,100.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who gets overtime pay in the USA?

Most non-exempt employees must be paid at least 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek under the Fair Labor Standards Act. Salaried exempt employees are usually not eligible.

Do all states use the same rule?

No. Some states, such as California, also require overtime after 8 hours in a single day and double time in some cases. Check your state labor department for exact rules.

What is double time?

Double time means paying 2 times the regular hourly rate. It is not required by federal law but applies in some states or union contracts after long shifts.

Is overtime taxed differently?

Overtime is taxed as regular income. It can push more of a paycheck into a higher withholding bracket, but it does not change your actual tax rate at filing time.
