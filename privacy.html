Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information EverydayCalc (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit this website and how it is used.

Numbers you enter in calculators

All calculations run in your web browser. The numbers you type into a calculator are not sent to our servers and are not stored by us.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the host that serves our pages may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to run the service. We use Google Search Console to see how our pages perform in Google Search. This gives us search queries and page performance in aggregate, not information about you personally.

Analytics

We may add a privacy-conscious analytics service in the future, such as Google Analytics, to understand which pages are useful. If we do, we will update this policy first.

Advertising

We may show ads from third-party networks such as Google AdSense in the future. These vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings. When we start showing ads, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

Cookies

EverydayCalc does not set its own cookies at this time. Third-party services we may use in the future can set cookies, as described above.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to other websites

We link to official sources such as government websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Please use our contact page.
